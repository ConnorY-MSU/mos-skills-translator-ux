Stage 3 · Walt Disney Creative Strategy

Same problem, three mindsets: Dreamer (anything goes), Realist (how could we build it), Critic (what could go wrong). Sticky notes digitized.

🌙 Dreamer

No limits. What would the perfect tool do for a Marine like Derek?
Say "I was a 0331" and the whole resume writes itself
Scan my DD-214 and auto-fill my entire career
AI mock interviewer that talks like a real hiring manager
Paste a job posting and it shows which of my experience matches
A vet who got out ahead of me reviews my resume
A "what I actually led" card: people, $ of gear, deployments
Works backwards too: pick a civilian job, see what I already qualify for

🛠 Realist

Which dreams can we actually build and trust, starting now?
Curated MOS → civilian title map using O*NET military crosswalk + veteran review
4-step guided wizard: MOS, duties, sensitivity, results
Duty chips and number steppers instead of a blank text box
Bullet templates written once by vets, filled with the user's numbers
STAR interview cards from the same duties
Phone-first. Derek applies from his phone between shifts
DD-214 scan and mock interviewer = later versions

🧐 Critic

Where does this break, backfire, or lose trust?
Inflated claims get vets caught in interviews. Must stay truthful.
Someone could type classified details. Never ask for specifics.
Generic keyword stuffing = the same TAP template problem again
AI-written text can sound fake or be wrong. Show the reasoning.
Vets distrust slick apps. Needs community endorsement.
Paying vet experts to validate every MOS is expensive
Too many steps and Derek quits halfway on his phone
